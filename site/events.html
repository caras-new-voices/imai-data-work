5. Trial-friction families (born 2026-06-30)

  • trial_{feature}_daily_limit_reached[_day{N}] / trial_{feature}_total_limit_reached / trial_feature_blocked — server-side from quota enforcement, deduped once per user+event per day.
  • trial_upgrade_<placement> (15 placements: top_bar, sidebar_workspace, search_banner, locked_403, …) / trial_unlock_modal_opened / trial_book_call_* / trial_sample_report_viewed — frontend via the whitelisted bridge. Aggregate with LIKE 'trial_upgrade_%'.
  • Any trend crossing 2026-06-30 is a measurement change. First read (July): 40% of trials hit the unlock modal; book-call CTAs fire ~3×/month (invisible CTA).
