5. Verified baselines (from the internal catalog, dated)

  • Trial 7 days; trial→paid median 6.9d, ~76% converting day 6–8 (autocharge); trial cancel median ~4h (mean ~39h).
  • Event log ≈70% of charges / 36% of recurring dollars (June 2026) — the reason dollars never come from webhook events.
  • "isActive" lies: ~2,170 raw vs ~650 real active (endTime+endTrial filter). Paying churn needs a prior-payment EXISTS filter (raw ~4× too high).
  • 561 manual payers; ~280 with zero events ever; health score at usage-board.service.ts:1909.
