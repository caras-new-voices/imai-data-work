5. Deploy & ops

  • Deploys: deploy/deploy.js → pm2 on GCE discovery-prod/-stag; announces to a Slack hook. No CI/CD pipeline in the repo.
  • Env conventions differ per service: backend/main gates on NODE_ENV==='prod', the proxy runs 'production' — a recurring source of dead config (Q22) and gate mistakes.
  • backend/main talks to TWO databases: its prod Postgres (TypeORM) and the pixel DB (PIXEL_DB_HOST/USER/PASSWORD/NAME, campaigns.service.ts:1114) — the pixel DB also ingests Phyllo creator-auth webhooks.
  • Observability: Sentry (frontend), pino SL_EVENTS → GCP log metrics (social-listening), Slack alert hooks ×5 families (quota blocks, cancellations, bad-card trials, mail failures, deploys) — all hardcoded in source today.
